# Photography Events 0.15.0

Four changes, all from watching the cards in use.

## Seen it

Events now carry a third choice beside Follow and Skip. **"Seen it · Got the shot"** puts an occurrence away for the rest of its season and says so on the row: *next year's window is a separate event and comes back on its own.*

That was already true mechanically — an occurrence key carries its year, so skipping one never touched the next — but nothing said so, and "Skip" is the wrong word for a photograph you have already taken. Skip is *not going*; Seen is *done*. Both hide the row; only the wording differs, and the wording was the whole gap.

## Sunsets get their own drive limit

A whale peak six hours away is a trip you plan. A sunset six hours away is nothing — it is decided on that afternoon's forecast, and nobody drives half a day for one. Listing them at the global radius trains you to scroll past the category, which costs the evenings that were worth stepping outside for.

Sunsets now default to **one hour**, configurable in the integration options. A per-category limit only ever tightens; it can never let through something the global limit excluded.

## The seven-day list is coloured by likelihood

Category colour answers a question nobody asks of five things happening this week — you can read the category off the row. The accent now tracks the score instead: green at 90+, amber 80–89, orange 70–79, grey below. A short legend says so.

The year calendar keeps category colour, where telling subjects apart is the entire point.

## Timeline is no longer a second name for the planning calendar

With the integration installed, an existing Timeline card renders the planning calendar — deliberate, so old dashboards keep working. But the mode picker was offering both, as if they were different cards. They are not. Timeline now appears only where it is genuinely a different view: a standalone installation with no planning sensor, or a config already saved with it.
